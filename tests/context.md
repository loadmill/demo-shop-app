Demo Shop is a fully offline Android store (package `com.loadmill.demoshop`). It needs no login and has no backend.

- The catalog lists Loadmill merch, such as the Backpack and the Water Bottle. Each product card has an "Add to cart" button.
- The cart is opened from the "Cart" button in the header. It has a "Checkout" button.
- The checkout form is prefilled with demo contact, shipping and card details. "Place order" submits it, and no real payment is made.
- After a successful order, the app shows an "Order confirmed" screen with an order number.
