# Credits

## Product imagery

The six fictional Loadmill merch images were generated with OpenAI image generation and exported as 800×800 WebP files in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`. The approved collection uses distinct designs and colors rather than the same logo placement on each product.

| File | Product | Design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `product_backpack.webp` | Backpack | Rust-orange ripstop with a tonal windmill pattern |
| `product_water_bottle.webp` | Water Bottle | Forest-green Stay in the Flow bottle |
| `product_jersey.webp` | Jersey | Blue Loadmill basketball jersey |
| `product_hat.webp` | Hat | Washed-black Ship it. Sleep well. cap |
| `product_headphones.webp` | Headphones | Plum-purple Focus Mode headphones |
| `product_tshirt.webp` | T-Shirt | Cream Let It Run graphic tee |

The jersey used the original T-shirt photo by [Avtar Singh](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-plain-white-t-shirt-hangs-on-a-wooden-hanger-8ACmRoleM24) as a photography and composition reference. The original catalog photos shown in the historical README screenshots came from Unsplash under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): backpack by [Wiser by the Mile](https://unsplash.com/photos/brown-leather-backpack-on-white-surface-3o-X8WJOP5E), bottle by [Joan Tran](https://unsplash.com/photos/green-bottle-on-white-table-reEySFadyJQ), sneakers by [mostafa mahmoudi](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-pair-of-running-shoes-on-a-white-background-W_uDEmTq0po), sunglasses by [Kiran CK](https://unsplash.com/photos/black-framed-sunglasses-on-white-surface-lSl94SZHRgA), headphones by [Ravi Palwe](https://unsplash.com/photos/white-wireless-headphones-on-white-table-ax23KYiUdJc), and T-shirt by Avtar Singh.

## Brand

"Loadmill Demo Shop" is the name of this demo app. Its wordmark and shopping-bag logo were drawn for this demo; the products and store are not real.

The Droid mark printed on the shopping bag in the launcher icon, the in-app logo and `docs/logo.svg` is a trademark of Loadmill, used with permission. It is recolored to a solid blue silhouette and bundled as `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ic_droid_mark.png`.
